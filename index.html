Yara M. Damaj

Political Theorist

Yara M. Damaj is a political theorist and PhD candidate in the Department of Political Science at the University of Pennsylvania. Her work sits at the intersection of critical theory, postcolonial thought, and affect theory, with a focus on the Middle East. Her dissertation examines how nostalgia operates as an affective structure shaping subjectivity in Lebanon under conditions of neoliberal capitalism and crisis.

Broadly, she is interested in questions of political subjectivity, historical materialism, feminist political thought, and psychoanalytic approaches to politics.

She holds an MA in Public Policy and International Affairs from the American University of Beirut, where she also completed her undergraduate studies in Psychology and Politics, with a minor in Human Rights and Transitional Justice.
